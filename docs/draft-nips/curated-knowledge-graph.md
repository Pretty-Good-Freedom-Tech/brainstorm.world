Curated Knowledge Graphs
=====

The ultimate purpose of web of trust is to build a _personalized knowledge graph_ (KG) that is curated by one's trusted community. The question arises: what are the organizational principles of the KG? The purpose of this NIP is to provide structure to the KG using a small handful of core principles, with the _class thread principle_ chief among them. We also discuss how the WoT can be used to establish additional organizational principles for the KG.
