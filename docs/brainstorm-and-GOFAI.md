
Most nostr clients today are firehoses. Notes fly past, some brilliant, most noise. We rely on manually-curated follows, mute lists, or relays with simplistic algos to filter signal. What if, instead, every user had their own living, evolving Knowledge Graph whose facts and edges were continuously ranked by a trust algorithm propagating through their personal web of trust? That is Brainstorm. It's what we're building at NosFabrica: a real product that solves real problems for real users in the real world.

## The AI Connection

It just so happens that academics have been trying -- and failing -- for decades to achieve almost exactly the same goal as Brainstorm. This academic program is called symbolic AI, can also go by the name of Good Old Fashioned AI (GOFAI). GOFAI’s holy grail (especially in the Cyc era) was a giant, hand-curated (or semi-automatically curated), logically consistent knowledge base that contains commonsense and domain knowledge in machine-readable form. Brainstorm’s personalized Knowledge Graph is exactly that—except the curation is distributed and trust-weighted rather than done by a central team of knowledge engineers.

GOFAI, by the way, is in an entirely different category than connectionist AI: neural networks, statistical learning, machine learning, etc. The LLMs that have attracted billions since 2017 has stolen attention from GOFAI, and not everyone is happy about that. Many academics, like Josh Tenenbaum, view GOFAI as the superior alternative to connectionist AI, the billions of dollars of investment a tragic misallocation of resources.
